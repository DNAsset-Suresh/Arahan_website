01Company background

Company background
& core strengths.

A proprietorship registered in Chennai, executing finishing and civil packages across Tamil Nadu and Gujarat for EPC contractors, power utilities, manufacturers and workspace operators.

Our capabilities extend from substrate preparation through final finishing, allowing multiple finishing trades to be delivered under one contract with a single point of accountability — backed by the civil capability to take up foundations, structures and infrastructure independently or as part of a combined scope.
