# Aarahan Enterprises — SEO Keyword Map

Keyword strategy for **arahanenterprises.com**, built from the services, sectors and locations in the Aarahan Enterprises business proposal.

**Two things to know before using this list**

1. **No search-volume data.** These keywords are derived from the actual scope of work and from how Indian B2B construction buyers normally search. They are *candidates*, not measured demand. Validate volume and competition in **Google Keyword Planner**, then let **Google Search Console** (Queries report) tell you what the site really ranks for after 8–12 weeks.
2. **The `<meta name="keywords">` tag does nothing for Google or Bing.** It has been ignored for years. The tag is already on every page and is harmless, but ranking comes from the **title, meta description, H1/H2, body copy, image alt text, internal link anchors, the Google Business Profile and structured data**. This document maps keywords to those places.

## 5. Where to actually place keywords

| Place | What to do |
|---|---|
| Title tag | Primary keyword first, brand last |
| H1 | One per page; may be a natural-language version of the primary keyword |
| H2 / H3 | Secondary keywords as real section headings — never stuffed |
| First 100 words | Use the primary keyword once, naturally |
| Image alt text | Describe the work: "epoxy flooring with line marking in a warehouse" — the site already does this; keep it when you swap in real photographs |
| Internal links | Use descriptive anchors ("civil and infrastructure capabilities"), not "click here" |
| URLs | Already clean and keyword-bearing (`civil-infrastructure.html`). Do not rename them now — you would lose any accrued value |
| Schema | The `GeneralContractor` block already carries name, address, phone, area served and a `knowsAbout` list — keep that list aligned with these keywords |

## 6. Local SEO (the highest-return action for this business)

Most of these searches are local and commercial. Do this before any content work:

1. **Create/claim the Google Business Profile** — primary category *General Contractor*; secondary *Flooring Contractor*, *Painter*, *Interior Fitting Contractor*.
2. **NAP consistency** — the name, address and phone must match the site exactly, everywhere:
   `Aarahan Enterprises · No. 9, Indira Nagar, 4th Cross Street, Athipattu, Chennai – 600 120 · +91 93819 40980`
3. **Service areas** — list the Chennai industrial belt and any Gujarat locations you genuinely service.
4. **Photos** — real site and project photographs, geotagged, added monthly.
5. **Reviews** — ask clients and site engineers; never buy or fabricate them.
6. **Directories** — IndiaMART, Justdial, Sulekha, TradeIndia and local business listings, each with identical NAP.

---

## 7. Keywords to avoid

Do not target terms the business cannot support — they fail, and some create legal or trust risk:

- "ISO certified", "government approved", "licensed engineer" — no such certification is evidenced in the proposal
- "best / No.1 / top civil contractor in Chennai" — unprovable superlatives
- "turnkey EPC contractor", "design and build", "architect" — different scope from what is offered
- Cities and states not actually serviced
- Client names in titles or ad copy implying endorsement (listing past organisations factually on the Clients page is fine)
- Rate-based keywords with invented prices
