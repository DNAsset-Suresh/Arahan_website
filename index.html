04Method

How we execute
the work.

A site engineer and supervisor are assigned to every contract for its full duration and remain the client’s single point of contact from mobilisation to handover.

  1. 01

    Mobilisation

    Site handover, gang deployment, material staging and safety induction within the agreed window.

  2. 02

    Samples & Mock-ups

    Material submittals and a mock-up panel or room approved before bulk work starts.

  3. 03

    Stage Execution

    Work sequenced area by area, with levels, joints and coats checked at each stage.

  4. 04

    Measurement & Billing

    Joint measurement with the client’s engineer; running bills with test records attached.

  5. 05

    Snag & Handover

    Snag list closed area by area, cleaning done, area handed over for occupation.
