02Combined scope

The civil capability
behind the finish.

Civil works are taken up independently or combined with our finishing trades. When a single contractor carries the work from foundations and structure through to the final coat, there are no gaps between agencies at handover.

  • Height and excavation work permits
  • Stage inspection of levels, joints and coats
  • Joint measurement with the client’s engineer
  • Measurement books and test records prepared to EPC and PSU formats
